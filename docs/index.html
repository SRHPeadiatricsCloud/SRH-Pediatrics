Hospital Bilirubin Nomograms

Public bedside calculator for four bilirubin decision charts now integrated in the SRH Pediatrics webapp: MRD/090 for birth weight under 1250 g, MRD/091 for infants under 35 weeks gestation, plus shared ≥35 week phototherapy and ≥35 week exchange-transfusion cut-off charts.

9.9 mg/dL
Examples from the supplied ≥35 week charts: isoimmune haemolytic disease, G6PD deficiency, asphyxia, significant lethargy, temperature instability, sepsis, acidosis, low albumin.
This public page is a lightweight deployment of the bilirubin nomogram feature. It recreates the shared source charts for bedside plotting; if a value sits right on a decision boundary, cross-check the original chart before acting.
