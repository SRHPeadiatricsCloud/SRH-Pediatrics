Hospital Phototherapy Nomograms

Public bedside calculator for the two hospital neonatal jaundice charts integrated in the SRH Pediatrics webapp: MRD/090 for birth weight under 1250 g and MRD/091 for infants under 35 weeks gestation. Enter age in hours, birth weight, gestation, TSB and blood groups to auto-select the line and plot the current point.
